# Profiles of schools proposed for closure

Sources: SY26 enrollment, building data and pathway from the district's Oct. 6, 2026 utilization workbook; demographics (NCES 2024-25) and MCA % proficient via SchoolDigger/allk12 (MDE data, approx.); EL and SpEd % from exploremps.org (older, approx.). Receiving schools and miles from the closure model.

## Heritage  (9-12, Districtwide Program pathway, Powderhorn)
- In: Optimum plan (5 closures); 3-closure plan. Also closed in district: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3
- SY26 enrollment 68 (193,441 sq ft, built 1976)
- Students: 100% students of color (Black 98.6; Hispanic 1.4); 89% free/reduced lunch; EL 49%; SpEd 4%
- Reading proficient: 17% (2026)
- Math proficient: 0% (2026)
- Program: STEM; Somali language & culture; PSEO/CTE
- Students go to: Edison (68 students, 7.4 road mi, same HS pathway; receiver reading n/a (HS)) (kept together in one building)
